# Промты для концепт-артов

По этим промтам сделаны арты в `art/concepts/`. Чтобы новые картинки совпадали по стилю, добавляйте в конец каждого промта:

```
Consistent art direction: realistic macro nature, warm cinematic light, slightly stylized insects.
```

Для другой локации или сезона меняйте только описание места: `winter, frost on grass`, `rainy night`, `sandy beach`, `kitchen floor`.

## Битва на поверхности → `02_battle.png`

```
Screenshot of a 3D real-time strategy PC game about ant colonies. Two ant armies of thousands clash in formation on a forest floor, seen from an elevated tactical camera. Blocks of soldier ants hold a line, acid-spraying ants fire from behind, a flanking squad moves through tall grass. Macro photography look: shallow depth of field, sunbeams through grass blades, dew drops, floating dust. Minimal clean RTS interface: unit cards at the bottom, minimap in the corner. Photorealistic, cinematic lighting, 16:9.
```

## Колония в разрезе → `01_colony_cross_section.png`

```
Screenshot of a cozy 3D colony-building strategy game. Cross-section view of an underground ant nest: winding tunnels and chambers with a food storage full of seeds, a nursery with larvae, a mushroom farm, an aphid barn and a queen's chamber. Warm glowing light in each chamber, soft earthy tones, tiny ants carrying food. Above ground: grass, autumn leaves, evening sun. Cozy, atmospheric, detailed, soft volumetric light, minimal resource bar at the top, 16:9.
```

## Карта участка → `04_campaign_map.png`

```
Strategic campaign map of a PC strategy game set in a garden, seen from above like a miniature diorama with tilt-shift blur. Marked locations: the player's anthill, two rival anthills in different colors, a fallen apple, a puddle, a stag beetle lair under a log, a flower bed with aphids.
```
